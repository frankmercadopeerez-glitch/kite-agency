Conditions in Cartagena change by hour and area. Keep the session window flexible and reconfirm by WhatsApp before travelling to the beach.

On the activity day

Check equipment, launch area, swimmers, obstacles and the safety procedure. If conditions do not match your level, the exercise changes or the session is rescheduled.
