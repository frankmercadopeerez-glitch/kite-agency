Practical guide

Cartagena combines tropical weather, urban access and a coast exposed to the trade winds. A good session is never chosen by the calendar alone: level, weight, kite size, direction, gusts, waves and available space must be assessed together.

If you are travelling from the United States, Canada or Europe, send your dates and riding level before building a day around one fixed session. We help you keep the plan flexible and confirm the meeting point by WhatsApp close to the date.

Use this guide to ask better questions and avoid surprises. It is not a live forecast and it never overrides an instructor’s decision or official marine warnings.

How to plan it

04

Before booking

Share dates, experience, approximate weight, party size and preferred language. This lets us suggest equipment and format instead of selling a generic package.

05

Before leaving

Check the CIOH–DIMAR bulletin, reconfirm your session and bring water-resistant sun protection, hydration and comfortable active clothing.

06

At the beach

Review the launch area, obstacles, swimmers and emergency plan. If conditions do not fit your level, the exercise changes or the session is rescheduled.

07

Afterwards

Record what you learned and the next objective. Consistent progression is more useful than skipping stages in one session.

Planning for international visitors

La Boquilla is north of Cartagena’s historic centre and Rafael Núñez airport. Actual transfer time depends on traffic and accommodation. Avoid scheduling a tight flight or connection immediately after a session; allow time for transfers, wind changes and rinsing equipment.

Official sources and forecast

Check DIMAR/CIOH marine forecasts and warnings before any activity. External information does not replace a beach assessment.

Questions about this guide

Can I arrange everything from the US or Europe?

Yes. We coordinate by WhatsApp before your trip; final confirmation remains subject to conditions and availability.

Where do sessions take place?

Our reference base is La Boquilla, Cartagena. The exact area is confirmed on the day according to conditions and local operations.

Can I pay on this website?

Not yet. We first confirm availability, scope, final price and the applicable policy in writing.
