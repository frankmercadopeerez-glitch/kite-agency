DIAGNOSIS · REPAIR · SAFETY

What we can inspect

  • Bladder and valve air leaks
  • Canopy tears and seam reinforcement
  • Lines, pigtails, bar and safety system
  • Visual equipment inspection before returning to the water

How to plan it well

We first document the damage with photos, brand, model and year. We then confirm feasibility, scope, estimated time and price before working on the equipment. A repair does not remove overall wear or replace a complete safety inspection. To verify and explore this point, see the IKO equipment-maintenance manual and the Duotone manufacturer technical notice.

Not every item is safely repairable. Severe UV degradation, incompatible critical parts or compromised safety systems may require replacement.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience?

No. Tell us it is your first time so the plan can be adapted.

Is wind guaranteed?

No. We confirm close to the session and may need to reschedule.

What is the best season?

December through April is usually the most consistent window, without a daily guarantee.

Where does it take place?

Our reference base is La Boquilla; the exact area is confirmed according to conditions.

Is equipment included?

It depends on the selected course or rental and is confirmed in writing before payment.
