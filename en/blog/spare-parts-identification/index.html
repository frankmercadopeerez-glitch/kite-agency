How to request the correct kite spare part

Prepare photographs and references for valves, connectors and other components instead of buying by resemblance.

Read the guide

Kite Cartagena editorial team · Reviewed: 2026-09-27

Short answer

Prepare photographs and references for valves, connectors and other components instead of buying by resemblance.

In this guide

  1. Identify the equipment before the part
  2. Show context and detail
  3. Similar does not mean compatible
  4. Confirm availability and delivery

Identify the equipment before the part

Start with the setup’s brand, model, year and size. Some spares differ between similarly named versions. If the reference is unknown, send a readable identification photograph rather than guessing the missing detail.

Show context and detail

Include an overall view locating the area and a close-up of the component. An isolated image can conceal how it connects to the rest. Describe the observed symptom and when it occurs separately from your theory about the cause.

Similar does not mean compatible

Two connectors may look alike but have different dimensions or specifications. Obtain supplier or manufacturer confirmation before purchasing. Also ask whether fitting requires technical work or additional parts so the repair budget is complete.

Confirm availability and delivery

Request the exact reference, estimated lead time and installation conditions. Mention an upcoming flight before authorising work. Receiving a spare does not mean the equipment has already been checked or is ready to return to the water.
