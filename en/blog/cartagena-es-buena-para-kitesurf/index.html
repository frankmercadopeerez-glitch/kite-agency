Short answer

Cartagena combines air access, warm water and several riding areas, but a useful session still depends on actual wind, sea state, available space and rider level. First-time visitors should confirm the location, time and plan with the school before travelling to the beach.

When Cartagena makes sense

Seasonal guidance helps select travel dates; it does not guarantee every day. Cartagena generally concentrates its main wind season between December and March. Even then, the time, exercise or date may need to change after reviewing the forecast and local conditions.

What a beginner should verify

Ask about depth, waves, wind direction, safety space and the ratio of students, instructors and kites. An attractive beach is not automatically a teaching area. The decision must match the precise meeting point and exercise planned for that day.

Turn the question into a usable booking

Send your dates, previous experience, approximate weight, number of participants and goal. Ask for duration, equipment, price, location and the no-conditions policy as separate answers. You can then decide whether Cartagena fits your trip without relying on a broad destination promise.
