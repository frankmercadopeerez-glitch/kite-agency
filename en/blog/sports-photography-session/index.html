Kitesurf photography: what to agree before booking

Define the goal, delivery and permissions without disrupting instruction or promising exact visual conditions.

Read the guide

Kite Cartagena editorial team · Reviewed: 2026-09-27

Short answer

Define the goal, delivery and permissions without disrupting instruction or promising exact visual conditions.

In this guide

  1. Choose the result you want
  2. Confirm the scope
  3. Conditions affect the images
  4. Clarify usage permissions

Choose the result you want

Distinguish holiday memories, social media images and footage for technical analysis. Each requires different framing and organisation. Explain if you are also taking a lesson, because a photographer should not change the instructor’s exercises.

Confirm the scope

Ask about duration, image quantity or selection criteria, editing, format and delivery time. A session price does not define these details by itself. Include video explicitly if you want it as well as photographs.

Conditions affect the images

Light, distance and how the activity develops influence the result. A promotional image does not promise you will perform the same manoeuvre. Photographer and participant positions must respect the session plan.

Clarify usage permissions

Agree how you receive files and whether publication through business channels is authorised. Distinguish purchasing images from consenting to advertising use. Identifiable companions also need a clear conversation about the intended use.
