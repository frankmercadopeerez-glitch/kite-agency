What your kitesurf booking confirmation should contain

Keep the service, participants, currency, schedule and conditions in one accessible record.

Read the guide

Kite Cartagena editorial team · Reviewed: 2026-09-27

Short answer

Keep the service, participants, currency, schedule and conditions in one accessible record.

In this guide

  1. Identify the purchased service
  2. Clarify the amount
  3. Save the agreed conditions
  4. Keep an accessible copy

Identify the purchased service

The confirmation should distinguish a course, refresher, rental or supervision. Include participants and duration, stating whether hours are individual or shared. A message containing only an amount does not adequately describe what you expect to receive.

Clarify the amount

Keep the currency, total, any deposit and remaining balance. Distinguish per-person from group rates. Record external services separately so transport or photography is not mistakenly treated as part of the main price.

Save the agreed conditions

Include how weather changes, personal cancellations and partial sessions are handled. Clarify anything unclear before paying. You need the conditions applying to your actual booking rather than a policy inferred from other websites.

Keep an accessible copy

Save or download the summary with the meeting point and contact. Share payment details only through agreed channels and avoid publishing receipts containing personal information. At the end of a package, check that the recorded hours match completed sessions.
