Your first wing session: how it differs from a kite lesson

A directly held wing

In wing sports, you hold the wing with your hands rather than control a kite connected by lines. This changes coordination and learning. Previous experience can provide references but does not remove the need to become familiar with the new setup.

Clarify the planned practice

Ask which board and configuration the introduction uses. The name wingfoil does not guarantee the whole first session happens elevated above the foil. Understanding the goal prevents comparing packages only through promotional images.

Share water-sport experience

Describe previous sports and recent board practice. The instructor can decide how this informs exercise selection. You do not need to buy a wing before the lesson; first understand equipment suited to your circumstances.

Assess how to continue

At the end, ask what you controlled and what remains unfinished. Distinguish wing familiarisation from independence with the complete setup. Check specific requirements for rental or independent practice instead of automatically applying kitesurf requirements.
