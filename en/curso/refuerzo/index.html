How it works

Recover after a break or solve one specific objective such as relaunch, upwind riding, transitions, power control or preparation for first jumps after a level check.

The hour starts with a quick diagnosis and one measurable objective. One skill is prioritised and the session ends with a concrete practice recommendation.

What is included

Private instruction

An instructor follows each attempt and adapts the next exercise to your actual progress and safety needs.

Level review

The session starts by checking control, safety release and the technical goal so time is not wasted repeating content.

One skill focus

The hour targets one agreed obstacle with exercises and feedback chosen specifically for that objective.

Hydration

Hydration is available during the activity; arrive rested and use suitable sun protection for Cartagena.

Refresher lesson

Who it is for

For experienced riders who do not need a complete beginner course but want concentrated professional observation and correction.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session. A session is not forced in unsuitable conditions. The exercise, time or area may change, or the activity may be rescheduled according to safety and availability.
