Intermediate course

A 4-hour course moving from basic kite control toward body drag, relaunch and first water-start attempts. Content is adapted to actual level and may be split according to conditions and progress.

How it works

Consolidate kite control and body drag, practise water relaunch and prepare or begin the water start. This course bridges a short introduction and the stage where board riding begins.

Four hours can be delivered in one or two blocks according to wind and energy. Existing skills are checked first, then the main technical obstacle is addressed.

What is included

Private instruction

An instructor follows each attempt and adapts the next exercise to your actual progress and safety needs.

Body drag

Learn controlled movement through the water and board recovery without relying on standing on the bottom.

Water start

Coordinate kite, board and body position to stand with control; each attempt is corrected before adding power.

Hydration

Hydration is available during the activity; arrive rested and use suitable sun protection for Cartagena.

Intermediate course

Who it is for

For students who completed an introduction or can control a trainer kite but still need close support to coordinate kite, board and body position.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session. To verify and explore this point, see IKO safety resources and the official DIMAR SIPSEM forecast.
