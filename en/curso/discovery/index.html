How it works

Learn the wind window, power generation, basic setup, safety release and the first sensations of controlled pull. Riding away after two hours is not promised.

The lesson starts on land with safety and control. If conditions and the instructor’s assessment allow it, the session moves into assisted body drag in the water.

What is included

Complete equipment

Kite, bar, board, harness, helmet and flotation are selected for your size, level and the day’s conditions.

Safety training

Practise the wind window, safe area, signals, quick release and recovery procedure before generating power.

Kite control

Work on steering, stopping, stability and progressive power before combining the kite with the board.

Body drag

Learn controlled movement through the water and board recovery without relying on standing on the bottom.

Who it is for

Ideal for someone who has never tried kitesurfing and wants a structured introduction before choosing a longer program.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session. To verify and explore this point, see IKO safety resources and the official DIMAR SIPSEM forecast.

Frequently asked questions: Initiation

What do we work on in Initiation?

Learn the wind window, power generation, basic setup, safety release and the first sensations of controlled pull. Riding away after two hours is not promised.

How is the duration of Initiation?

The lesson starts on land with safety and control. If conditions and the instructor’s assessment allow it, the session moves into assisted body drag in the water.

What is included in Initiation?

The page shows the main inclusions. Before payment we confirm equipment size and availability, hydration, support, and any applicable transfer or extra in writing.

What happens when wind is unsuitable for Initiation?

A session is not forced in unsuitable conditions. The exercise, time or area may change, or the activity may be rescheduled according to safety and availability.

Where is the meeting point for Initiation?

The main reference is Kitesurf en Cartagena School in La Boquilla. The exact location and any transfer are confirmed close to the activity because they depend on wind and format.
