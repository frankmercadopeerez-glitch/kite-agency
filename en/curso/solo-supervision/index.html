What is included

Setup assistance

We help assemble and check your own equipment while its history and internal condition remain your responsibility.

Launch and landing

Receive coordinated launch and landing assistance using clear signals and a prior area check.

Storage

A beach-side point is coordinated to organise and protect equipment outside the water during the booked time.

Hydration

Hydration is available during the activity; arrive rested and use suitable sun protection for Cartagena.

Supervision with own gear

Who it is for

For independent riders visiting an unfamiliar spot with their own gear. It does not include a full technical lesson.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session. To verify and explore this point, see IKO safety resources and DIMAR marine bulletins.
