How it works

Level, weight, wind and size are checked before delivery. Navigation limits and support signals are agreed; a lesson is proposed if independence cannot be demonstrated.

What is included

Complete equipment

Kite, bar, board, harness, helmet and flotation are selected for your size, level and the day’s conditions.

Launch and landing

Receive coordinated launch and landing assistance using clear signals and a prior area check.

Safety watch

Beach observation provides support if wind, equipment or traffic conditions change during the session.

Hydration

Hydration is available during the activity; arrive rested and use suitable sun protection for Cartagena.

Gear rental + supervision

Who it is for

Only for independent riders able to ride upwind, relaunch and use the safety system. Assistance is not a substitute for a lesson.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session. To verify and explore this point, see the IKO equipment-maintenance manual and the Duotone manufacturer technical notice.

What is included in Gear rental + supervision?

The page shows the main inclusions. Before payment we confirm equipment size and availability, hydration, support, and any applicable transfer or extra in writing.

What happens when wind is unsuitable for Gear rental + supervision?

A session is not forced in unsuitable conditions. The exercise, time or area may change, or the activity may be rescheduled according to safety and availability.

Where is the meeting point for Gear rental + supervision?

The main reference is Kitesurf en Cartagena School in La Boquilla. The exact location and any transfer are confirmed close to the activity because they depend on wind and format.
