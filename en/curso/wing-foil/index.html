Wing Foil lesson

A wing foil introduction with wing, foil board, helmet and vest, working on balance, wing handling and assisted foil take-off when appropriate.

How it works

Learn wing handling, hand position, board balance and forward drive before attempting foil take-off.

The hour moves from land or stable-board drills toward sailing and basic pumping when appropriate, with helmet and vest included in the safety approach.

What is included

Wing and foil board

Wing and foil board are configured for a progressive introduction based on weight, balance and wind.

Helmet and vest

Helmet and vest are fitted before entering; they do not replace suitable conditions and location.

Balance drills

Exercises begin with stance, wing direction and weight transfer before sustained foil flight is attempted.

Hydration

Hydration is available during the activity; arrive rested and use suitable sun protection for Cartagena.

Wing Foil lesson

Who it is for

For adults comfortable in the water; previous kitesurf experience helps but is not always required. Wind, weight and board stability shape the plan.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session.
