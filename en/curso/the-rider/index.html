The Rider

A complete zero-to-independence course covering wind theory, safety systems, setup, power control, body drag and first water starts. The 8 hours are normally split over 2–4 days according to energy and conditions.

How it works

Build a complete foundation for controlled kite handling, board recovery through body drag and first water starts without skipping the safety systems. The aim is to understand each decision, not simply copy movements.

Eight hours are usually split across two to four days. Every block starts with a short review and ends with a clear next objective; wind, fatigue and learning pace determine the final schedule.

What is included

Complete equipment

Kite, bar, board, harness, helmet and flotation are selected for your size, level and the day’s conditions.

Private instruction

An instructor follows each attempt and adapts the next exercise to your actual progress and safety needs.

Hydration

Hydration is available during the activity; arrive rested and use suitable sun protection for Cartagena.

Safety training

Practise the wind window, safe area, signals, quick release and recovery procedure before generating power.

The Rider

Who it is for

For first-time students seeking continuity and for learners who took a short lesson but do not yet control the kite and release system safely.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session. To verify and explore this point, see IKO safety resources and the official DIMAR SIPSEM forecast.

How is the duration of The Rider?

What is included in The Rider?

The page shows the main inclusions. Before payment we confirm equipment size and availability, hydration, support, and any applicable transfer or extra in writing.

What happens when wind is unsuitable for The Rider?

A session is not forced in unsuitable conditions. The exercise, time or area may change, or the activity may be rescheduled according to safety and availability.

Where is the meeting point for The Rider?

The main reference is Kitesurf en Cartagena School in La Boquilla. The exact location and any transfer are confirmed close to the activity because they depend on wind and format.
