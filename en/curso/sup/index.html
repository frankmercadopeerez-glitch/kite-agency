How it works

Enjoy a calm stand-up paddle outing with basic equipment and area guidance at an appropriate time.

Board, paddle and vest are delivered with posture, turning and return guidance. The route stays close to a suitable area and may change for safety.

What is included

SUP board and paddle

Includes SUP board and paddle plus a basic stance and paddle-length adjustment before departure.

Life vest

A suitable flotation vest is provided and fitted before entering the water.

Route guidance

The suggested route considers wind, current, traffic, return point and areas to avoid.

Hydration

Hydration is available during the activity; arrive rested and use suitable sun protection for Cartagena.

SUP rental

Who it is for

For beginners and recreational paddlers who are comfortable in the water. Waves, wind, current and beach traffic still determine availability.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session. To verify and explore this point, see the official DIMAR SIPSEM forecast and DIMAR marine bulletins.

Frequently asked questions: SUP rental

What do we work on in SUP rental?

Enjoy a calm stand-up paddle outing with basic equipment and area guidance at an appropriate time.

How is the duration of SUP rental?

Board, paddle and vest are delivered with posture, turning and return guidance. The route stays close to a suitable area and may change for safety.

What is included in SUP rental?

The page shows the main inclusions. Before payment we confirm equipment size and availability, hydration, support, and any applicable transfer or extra in writing.

What happens when wind is unsuitable for SUP rental?

A session is not forced in unsuitable conditions. The exercise, time or area may change, or the activity may be rescheduled according to safety and availability.

Where is the meeting point for SUP rental?

The main reference is Kitesurf en Cartagena School in La Boquilla. The exact location and any transfer are confirmed close to the activity because they depend on wind and format.
