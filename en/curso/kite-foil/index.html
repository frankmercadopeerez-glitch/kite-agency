How it works

Understand mast and board setup, foil height and pitch control, efficient stance and progressive short flights.

Experience, depth and conditions are checked before setup. Drills progress from taxi control to short flights without unnecessary speed or height.

What is included

Foil board setup

Board, mast, fuselage, wings and hardware are checked and configured for the first learning stage.

Technical coaching

Coaching builds on prior kite experience and targets stance, height control and safer falls.

Safety equipment

Helmet, flotation and the appropriate leash are prepared together with a check of the practice area.

Hydration

Hydration is available during the activity; arrive rested and use suitable sun protection for Cartagena.

Kitefoil lesson

Who it is for

Only for independent kiters who can ride upwind, control the kite one-handed and self-rescue. This is not a first kitesurf lesson.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Effective time is organized for safe, useful progression; the exact area and time are confirmed close to the session.
