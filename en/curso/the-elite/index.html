How it works

Complete a private progression with priority attention, video analysis, coordinated spot transfers and professional photography while reducing travel friction.

Twelve hours are scheduled in several blocks around the forecast. Extras are confirmed before booking, and unsuitable conditions are rescheduled rather than used for an unproductive session.

What is included

Private instruction

An instructor follows each attempt and adapts the next exercise to your actual progress and safety needs.

Video analysis

Short clips reveal posture, timing and decisions that can be difficult to notice while riding.

Spot transport

Included transport is coordinated in advance for the selected spot, conditions and confirmed plan.

Professional photos

Action photography is coordinated to document the session without interrupting the learning progression.

Elite VIP intensive

Who it is for

For travellers dedicating several days to kitesurfing who value private coaching and want lessons, transport, hydration, photos and video coordinated together.

Before confirmation we review experience, goal, approximate weight, dates and conditions. Frequently asked questions: Elite VIP intensive

What do we work on in Elite VIP intensive?

Complete a private progression with priority attention, video analysis, coordinated spot transfers and professional photography while reducing travel friction.

How is the duration of Elite VIP intensive?

Twelve hours are scheduled in several blocks around the forecast. Extras are confirmed before booking, and unsuitable conditions are rescheduled rather than used for an unproductive session.

What is included in Elite VIP intensive?

The page shows the main inclusions. Before payment we confirm equipment size and availability, hydration, support, and any applicable transfer or extra in writing.

What happens when wind is unsuitable for Elite VIP intensive?

A session is not forced in unsuitable conditions. The exercise, time or area may change, or the activity may be rescheduled according to safety and availability.

Where is the meeting point for Elite VIP intensive?

The main reference is Kitesurf en Cartagena School in La Boquilla. The exact location and any transfer are confirmed close to the activity because they depend on wind and format.
