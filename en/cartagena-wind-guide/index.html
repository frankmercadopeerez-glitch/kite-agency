Frequently asked questions

What is the best season?

December through April is usually the most consistent window, without a daily guarantee.

Where does it take place?

Our reference base is La Boquilla; the exact area is confirmed according to conditions.

Is equipment included?

It depends on the selected course or rental and is confirmed in writing before payment.

Can I book from abroad?

Yes. Send dates, level, approximate weight and party size by WhatsApp.

What if the weather changes?

The exercise, time or date is adapted according to safety and availability.
