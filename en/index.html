Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience?

No. Tell us it is your first time so the plan can be adapted. Clarify whether you want a first experience, technical progression, supervised practice or independence. Each goal requires a different format and amount of support.

Is wind guaranteed?

No. We confirm close to the session and may need to reschedule. Seasonality is only a starting point. Wind, waves, tide, usable space and beach traffic must be checked again for the specific day and area. The safest choice may be to adapt the exercise, change the time or reschedule. That is part of responsible operations, not a service failure.

What is the best season?

December through April is usually the most consistent window, without a daily guarantee. Size, condition, compatibility and the safety system must suit the rider and the conditions. Availability alone is not enough. The safest choice may be to adapt the exercise, change the time or reschedule. That is part of responsible operations, not a service failure.

Where does it take place?

Our reference base is La Boquilla; the exact area is confirmed according to conditions. Confirm meeting point, transport, effective duration, weather policy, inclusions, final price and contact channel before travelling.
