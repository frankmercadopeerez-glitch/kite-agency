A well-informed decision

A strong experience begins before reaching the beach. Compare your goal, actual level, available time, conditions and the service scope. This guide turns those variables into a practical process that prevents mismatched expectations and supports a useful booking decision.

Goal

Clarify whether you want a first experience, technical progression, supervised practice or independence. Each goal requires a different format and amount of support.

Conditions

Seasonality is only a starting point. Wind, waves, tide, usable space and beach traffic must be checked again for the specific day and area.

Equipment

Size, condition, compatibility and the safety system must suit the rider and the conditions. Availability alone is not enough.

Logistics

Confirm meeting point, transport, effective duration, weather policy, inclusions, final price and contact channel before travelling.
