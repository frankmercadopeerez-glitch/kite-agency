Who We Are

Built on Discipline.
Driven by Passion.

A Team of Professionals

Kite Cartagena is not just a school; it is a standard. Founded in 2020 by Frank Mercado, a Naval Consultant and former Navy Officer, our agency was born from a clear vision: to elevate water sports tourism in Cartagena.

While Frank leads the strategic vision, Kite Cartagena is a team effort. We are a collective of IKO-certified instructors, professional athletes, and maritime safety experts. We saw visitors receiving subpar experiences with questionable safety measures, and we knew we could do better.

We combine high-performance equipment with military-grade planning. Whether you are kitesurfing, diving, or exploring the islands, you are backed by a team that understands the ocean's power and respects it as much as you do.

Our Philosophy

"We don't sell hours on the water. We sell confidence, adrenaline, and memories, wrapped in the highest safety standards in the Caribbean."
